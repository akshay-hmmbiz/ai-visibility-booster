5. THIRD-PARTY ACCESS TO ENDPOINT DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This plugin creates publicly accessible endpoints on your website:

    /ai-sitemap.xml
    /ai-feed.json
    /ai-knowledge.json
    /ai-markdown/{post-slug}

These endpoints expose the same content that is already publicly accessible on
your website (published post titles, URLs, summaries, taxonomy terms, and site
metadata). Draft, private and password-protected posts are left out. No private
user data or authentication credentials are exposed through these endpoints.

By activating this plugin, you acknowledge that:
  - These endpoints are accessible to any web crawler, bot, or automated system.
  - The plugin developer is not responsible for how third parties use data
    obtained from these endpoints.
  - You are responsible for complying with your jurisdiction's data protection
    laws regarding any personal data that may appear in your public post content.

6. PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This plugin has been designed with privacy in mind:
  - Author names are excluded from AI feed and knowledge graph endpoints.
  - No data is sent to any external server by this plugin.
  - Bot Analytics logs requests from known bot user agents only. It stores the
    user agent, page URL, time and a salted SHA-256 hash of the IP address in
    your own database. Raw IP addresses are not stored. Normal visitors are
    not logged.
  - The AI Visibility Score REST API requires a logged-in user who can edit
    the requested post.

However, the plugin developer is not responsible for ensuring that your website's
content complies with GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA, or any other data protection regulation.
You are responsible for auditing your published content for compliance with
applicable privacy laws.
